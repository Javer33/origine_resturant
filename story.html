The story behind the name

ORIGINE means
where it began.

We built a restaurant around one simple belief: food becomes more memorable when you understand the journey that brought it to you.

A modern kitchen with an old idea

Cook the present. Respect the past.

ORIGINE was imagined by Chef Elena Laurent after years of moving between European kitchens, Middle Eastern markets and South Asian home tables. The common thread was never technique alone — it was the story carried by an ingredient.

Our plates are contemporary, but their starting points are real: saffron from Kashmir, black lime from the Gulf, apricot from the old Silk Road, rose and cardamom from South Asian kitchens.

Our design ruleIf an ingredient cannot tell us something about place, season or memory, it does not need to be on the menu.
Chef Elena Laurent

The chef who cooks in footnotes.

Elena treats every menu like a travel journal. She sketches, tastes, researches, then removes anything that feels decorative rather than meaningful.

Her signature is restraint: one clear ingredient, one unexpected connection, and enough space for the guest to finish the sentence.
