The ORIGINE philosophy

Before a dish reaches the table,
it has already travelled.

“Every spice has a birthplace. Every recipe has a memory. We cook with both.”

Our menu follows ingredients back to their roots, then brings them into a contemporary dining language.

Expect familiar aromas in unfamiliar compositions — never for novelty, always for meaning.

Our promise No anonymous dishes. We tell you where the idea began, what is on your plate, how it tastes and what you should know before ordering.

The hands behind the table

Chef Elena Laurent

Elena's cooking began between a family kitchen and a notebook full of market sketches.

Her menus now move between European technique, South Asian spice craft and the quiet discipline of seasonal cooking.

“I want a guest to taste something beautiful — then become curious enough to ask where it came from.”
