The ORIGINE philosophy

Before a dish reaches the table, it has already travelled.

“Every spice has a birthplace. Every recipe has a memory. We cook with both.”

Our menu follows ingredients back to their roots, then brings them into a contemporary dining language. Expect familiar aromas in unfamiliar compositions — never for novelty, always for meaning.

Chef Elena Laurent

Elena's cooking began between a family kitchen and a notebook full of market sketches. Her menus now move between European technique, South Asian spice craft and the quiet discipline of seasonal cooking.
